Lightweight Trust & Monotonic Blockchain Policy for Critical IoT Firmware

BLOCKHACK 2026 Track: Blockchain Security & IoT

Main User Workflow

1
Vendor Builds & Signs
Vendor generates firmware binary, signs with Ed25519 private key, and submits SHA-256 metadata on-chain.
2
Auditor Role Verification
Independent Security Auditor reviews vulnerability disclosures and approves or revokes release on-chain.
3
Ledger Monotonic Policy
Smart contract checks versionCode >= device.minAcceptedVersion. Strict monotonic rule prevents rollback.
4
Field IoT Device Flashing
Gateway queries ledger proof; device verifies signature & burns monotonic OTP eFuse upon successful authorization.
